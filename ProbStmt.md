Problem Statement: Modern road networks suffer from reactive incident response, unmonitored surface hazards (potholes, flash floods, and accidents), speeding violations, and parking-related bottlenecks that compromise commuter safety and traffic flow. There is a need for an integrated monitoring system, Hawk Eye, capable of detecting road hazards, alerting drivers to speed limits, and identifying parking violations and vehicle number plates to support proactive route diversions, enhance road safety, and reduce speeding penalties.
